Telegram payments on NEAR

Give AI the brakes,
not the keys.

Pay someone new and the money waits in a vault that has no keys. While it waits, deterministic rules and open-weight AI models check the payment. If something looks wrong they make the wait longer. They can never move your money, and nobody can release it early.

How a payment works

  1. You pay

    Type send 50 {{ symbol }} to friend.testnet. Forward the message that asked you to pay, and it is checked too.

  2. It waits

    Payments to someone you have not trusted yet sit in HoldVault for a short wait. Trusted payees are paid at once, up to a daily cap.

  3. Checks add time

    Rules react within seconds. AI models dig deeper with web search, page fetches, code execution and on-chain intel. A flag extends the hold and records the reasons on-chain.

  4. You decide

    Cancel and the money comes back. Do nothing and it releases when the time is up. A guardian you chose can cancel for you.

Why it is safe

No keys to steal

The vault contract has zero access keys: no upgrades, no admin, no way out for the money except its own rules.

The AI can only brake

The oracle's single key can call extend_hold, which only makes a hold longer, never shorter, capped at 72 hours.

Reports travel

Every cancel after a warning is counted on-chain against the payee, so the next sender is braked by a rule within seconds.

Every reason is public

Each brake links to a timeline and an evidence JSON whose sha256 is stored with the hold. No message text ever goes on-chain.
